What's included

Every day is planned, so a new teacher can run it as written.

  • A slide deck for every lesson. 90 decks with bell ringers, exit tickets and speaker notes you can read aloud.
  • Teacher guides with minute-by-minute plans. Objectives, vocabulary, differentiation notes and answer keys for every unit.
  • Student materials. Guided notes, worksheets and Google Sheets calculators for unit economics, break-even and a 12-month projection.
  • Assessments. Unit tests and quizzes, a midterm and a final with keys, and question banks ready for Google Forms, Kahoot or Quizizz.

The Venture Project

Teams build one business all semester, then pitch it to community judges.

Each milestone becomes a section of the business plan, so by Week 16 most of the plan is already written. Teams of two to four sign a team contract, and teammates rate each other twice a semester.

Questions

Common questions from teachers and CTE directors

What do students need?

A school device with Google Docs, Slides and Sheets, and a free Canva for Education account that the teacher sets up for the class.

Can it fit our calendar?

Yes. The pacing calendar fills in real dates from your first day and no-school days, and marks the review and studio days you can compress first.

How does the course handle AI?

The syllabus has an AI section the teacher completes, and the teacher decides for each assignment whether and how AI tools may be used.

What happens on Pitch Day?

Teams pitch to community judges the teacher recruits through the school's visitor process. A judge packet includes the rubric, score sheets and time cards.

Does it meet a graduation requirement?

It is a business CTE elective. It supports several Minnesota economics benchmarks but is not by itself an economics credit.

What if our license lapses?

The license covers one school year. If it is not renewed, teaching with the materials ends June 30 (a class already under way may finish), and updates, monthly lessons, competition entry and onboarding stop. The full terms are in the one-page license agreement.
